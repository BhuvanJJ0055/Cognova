===================================================================
Cognova GenAI Platform - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
===================================================================

1. What is Cognova?
Cognova is an enterprise-grade, multi-page GenAI assistant ecosystem.
It integrates sentiment-aware customer support, medical Q&A with MedQuAD,
ArXiv computer science research paper summarization, multimodal image/document analysis,
and multilingual natural language translation.

2. How does Medical Q&A work?
The Medical Q&A module uses TF-IDF retrieval over MedQuAD datasets combined with
rule-based medical entity recognition (detecting diseases, symptoms, and treatments).
If an upset user is detected, empathetic de-escalation is automatically applied.

3. How does ArXiv Expert paper retrieval work?
ArXiv Expert connects to both live ArXiv APIs and local TF-IDF paper indices to extract
key concepts, summarize research papers, and generate structured explanations.

4. Can I add custom documents to the Knowledge Base?
Yes! Simply place raw text or CSV files inside the data/incoming_docs/ folder or use
the interactive Knowledge Base Updater UI on the Medical Q&A page.
